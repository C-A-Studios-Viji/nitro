Before you say hello
A few useful answers.
How do I connect with a mentor?
Join the WhatsApp community and introduce yourself, or email N!TRO using the form above. Describe your area of interest and the guidance you need. Connections depend on who is available; joining does not automatically book a mentoring session.
Can I offer mentorship or collaborate?
Yes. Choose “Offer mentorship” or “Collaborate with N!TRO” in the introduction builder. Share your experience, the areas you can help with and the kind of involvement you have in mind.
What should I include in my first message?
Your name, your team or project, what you have tried and one clear question. Keep sensitive information private. If you are a student, involve a parent or teacher when arranging a meeting.
Does this website send my email for me?
No. The introduction builder prepares a draft in your email app. You review it and send it yourself. You can also copy the introduction or use the direct email links.
